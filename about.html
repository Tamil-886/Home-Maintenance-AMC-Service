Our Story

How a frustration with traditional home repair chaos sparked a regional movement in proactive residential care.

Re-Engineering Home Maintenance From the Ground Up

In 2011, our founders—former facility directors responsible for multi-million dollar hospital and aviation facilities—observed a stark paradox: while commercial towers enjoyed predictive care and zero-downtime maintenance, residential homeowners were left navigating an unregulated, stressful repair marketplace.

Whenever a circuit tripped in winter or an air conditioner failed during a sweltering July heatwave, families were forced to scour directories for unvetted handymen, endure exorbitant emergency fees, and gamble on substandard craftsmanship.

HomeCarely was created to pioneer the Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) model for private residences. By replacing panicked, expensive emergency visits with structured, routine seasonal checkups and guaranteed priority response, we transformed residential care into an effortless, predictable experience.
