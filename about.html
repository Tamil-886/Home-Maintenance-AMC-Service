Our Journey & Purpose

Redefining How Modern Families Protect Their Homes

Founded in 2011, HomeCarely began with one simple observation: homeowners spend endless hours finding unreliable technicians during stressful emergencies, only to be hit with exorbitant bills and poor craftsmanship.

We introduced the comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) model to residential spaces. With predictable subscription tiers, scheduled preventive tune-ups, and verified in-house technicians, we eliminate home stress entirely.
