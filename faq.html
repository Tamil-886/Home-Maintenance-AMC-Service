An AMC is a prepaid annual service package that protects your home’s essential operating systems — including air conditioning, plumbing, electrical circuits, and sanitization. Instead of paying hefty inspection fees and emergency labor charges each time something breaks, an AMC covers regular preventive tune-ups, unlimited labor for repairs, and guaranteed rapid response windows.

The Standard Plan ($349/yr) includes 4 preventive maintenance visits per year and a 4-hour emergency response window. The Premium Plan ($599/yr) doubles scheduled tune-ups to 8 visits, adds unlimited emergency callouts with a strict 60-minute arrival guarantee, and includes 2 sessions of pest control and water tank cleaning.
